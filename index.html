GymLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

GymLog is designed with one principle: your data is yours.

Data Collection

GymLog collects no personal data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any identifying information. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no registration of any kind.

Data Storage

All data you enter into GymLog — workouts, exercises, progress, settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your phone. It is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Third-Party Services

GymLog uses Capgo for over-the-air app updates. Capgo delivers app updates to your device but does not collect, access, or process any of your personal or workout data. No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are present in this app.

Permissions

GymLog requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications — used only for the rest timer alert during workouts. Optional and can be declined.
  • Storage — used only to export your workout backup file when you choose to do so.

Children

GymLog does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
